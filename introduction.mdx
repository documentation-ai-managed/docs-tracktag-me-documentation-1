## Key Features

Tracktag.me offers a range of features designed to meet the needs of professional audio creators:

- **Imperceptible Watermarks**: Our advanced algorithms embed watermarks that are inaudible to human listeners but detectable by our verification tools.
- **Multiple Formats Support**: Compatible with WAV, MP3, AAC, and other popular audio formats.
- **Batch Processing**: Process entire libraries of tracks simultaneously.
- **Custom Watermarking**: Embed unique identifiers, timestamps, and metadata.
- **Cross-Platform**: Available for both Mac and Windows systems.
